## Dataset citations

For papers or presentations using these IMD products, please cite the relevant
publication below in addition to acknowledging the India Meteorological
Department (IMD).

### Rainfall · 0.25° × 0.25° — Pai et al. (2014)

Pai D.S., Latha Sridhar, Rajeevan M., Sreejith O.P., Satbhai N.S. and
Mukhopadhyay B., 2014:
[Development of a new high spatial resolution (0.25° × 0.25°) long period
(1901–2010) daily gridded rainfall data set over India and its comparison with
existing data sets over the region](https://www.imdpune.gov.in/cmpg/Griddata/ref_paper_MAUSAM.pdf).
*MAUSAM*, **65**, 1 (January 2014), pp. 1–18.

### Temperature — Srivastava et al. (2009)

A. K. Srivastava, M. Rajeevan and S. R. Kshirsagar, 2009:
[Development of High Resolution Daily Gridded Temperature Data Set (1969–2005)
for the Indian Region](https://www.imdpune.gov.in/cmpg/Griddata/Research%20paper.pdf).
*Atmospheric Science Letters* (Atmos. Sci. Let.).
DOI: [10.1002/asl.232](https://doi.org/10.1002/asl.232).
